# 🚀 FootPulse: Smart Kinetic Energy Harvester & IoT Monitor (V1.0)

**FootPulse** is a high-performance Mechatronics system designed to scavenge wasted kinetic energy from human footfalls. By integrating **Structural Dynamics** with **Power Electronics**, the system converts mechanical pulses into regulated electrical energy, monitored in real-time via an **ESP32-based IoT ecosystem**.

---

## 🏗️ 1. Mechanical Dynamics & Vibration Engineering
The system is engineered as a **Single Degree of Freedom (SDOF)** model, focusing on energy capture during the impulse and rapid restoration.

### **Mathematical Modeling**
The platform's movement is defined by the classical second-order differential equation:
$$m\ddot{x} + c\dot{x} + kx = F(t)$$

### **Numerical Parameters:**
* **Mass ($m$):** $5 \, \text{kg}$ (Upper Platform).
* **Total Stiffness ($k_{total}$):** $700.7 \, \text{N/m}$ (Calculated from $0.07\text{m}$ deflection).
* **Individual Spring Stiffness ($k_{spring}$):** $175.2 \, \text{N/m}$ (4 Parallel industrial springs).
* **Natural Frequency ($f_n$):** $1.88 \, \text{Hz}$ (Optimized for human walking cadence).

### **Damping Characterization (The Friction Strategy)**
To control the system's transient response, we utilized **Coulomb Damping** generated by structural bolts.
* **Critical Damping ($C_c$):** $118.4 \, \text{N.s/m}$
* **Actual Damping Coefficient ($c$):** $17.76 \, \text{N.s/m}$
* **Damping Ratio ($\zeta$):** $0.15$ (**Underdamped Case**)

> **Why Underdamped?** We selected $\zeta = 0.15$ to ensure a fast **Settling Time**, allowing the rack-and-pinion to reset quickly for the next footfall pulse while preventing gear-teeth wear.

---

## ⚡ 2. Electrical Power Stage & Circuitry
The "Pulse" from the generator is processed through a multi-stage power conditioning circuit.

### **The Power Path:**
1.  **Generation:** 12V 210RPM DC Generator driven by a $20\text{cm}$ Rack and $6\text{cm}$ Pinion.
2.  **Rectification:** Full-bridge rectifier + Filtering capacitors to eliminate ripples.
3.  **Regulation:** **Step-Down Converter** stabilizes the input for storage and control logic.
4.  **The Switching Matrix:** * **BJT (2N2222):** Operates in **Common Emitter** mode. 
    * **Measured Metrics:** $I_B = 2.5 \, \text{mA}$, $I_C = 12.5 \, \text{mA}$, ensuring **Deep Saturation**.
    * **MOSFET:** Final power stage for high-efficiency load switching with minimal $R_{DS(on)}$ losses.

---

## 🌐 3. IoT & Control Intelligence (The ESP32 Brain)
The system operates as a **Smart Node** in a wider monitoring network.

* **Real-time Sensing:** Voltage Divider feedback to the ESP32 ADC.
* **Decision Logic:** Smart thresholding (System triggers only at $V_{gen} \ge 2.0\text{V}$).
* **IoT Connectivity:** * **Bluetooth Serial:** Real-time data streaming to mobile devices (VibTronics_Project).
    * **LCD Display:** Local monitoring of system health and voltage output.
